# Amazon Marketplace Executive Dashboard | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Tableau executive dashboard** developed to analyze Amazon Marketplace sales performance and transform transactional data into clear, decision-focused business insights.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators, revenue trends, product performance, category performance, and order fulfillment while allowing users to dynamically explore the data using interactive filters.

This is **Phase 2 of my Amazon Marketplace Business Analytics project**, extending the SQL analysis into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide business stakeholders with a centralized view of marketplace performance and answer key questions such as:

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the strongest revenue contributors?
- What is the distribution of orders across fulfillment statuses?
- How does performance change across countries, years, categories, and order statuses?

---

## Executive KPIs

The dashboard tracks four primary business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Revenue** | Overall revenue generated from marketplace orders |
| **Total Orders** | Total number of orders recorded |
| **Total Customers** | Number of unique customers |
| **Average Order Value** | Average revenue generated per order |

---

## Dashboard Analysis

### Monthly Revenue Trend

Analyzes revenue performance over time to identify monthly fluctuations and overall sales patterns.

### Revenue by Category

Compares revenue contribution across product categories, helping identify the strongest-performing segments of the marketplace.

### Top 10 Products by Revenue

Ranks the highest-performing products based on total revenue, making it easier to identify major revenue contributors.

### Order Distribution

Analyzes the distribution of orders across different fulfillment statuses, including:

- Delivered
- Shipped
- Pending
- Returned
- Cancelled

This provides visibility into order fulfillment and operational performance.

---

## Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes dynamic filters that allow users to explore performance by:

- **Category**
- **Year**
- **Order Status**
- **Country**

These filters dynamically update the KPIs and visualizations, enabling users to perform targeted analysis across different business segments.

---

## Key Business Insights

- Revenue is distributed relatively evenly across major product categories, indicating a diversified marketplace revenue base.
- Electronics emerges as the highest revenue-generating category in the overall dataset.
- Top-performing products contribute similar revenue levels, suggesting revenue is not heavily dependent on a single product.
- Delivered orders represent the majority of overall order activity.
- Interactive filtering enables deeper analysis of performance across countries, categories, years, and fulfillment stages.
